PROBLEM STATEMENT
The Industry-Academia Placement Disconnect
01. Lack of Early Forecasting
Students realize placement readiness gaps in final semester when it is too late to pivot or bridge fundamentals.
02. Ambiguous Benchmarks
Job descriptions are noisy and vague; students struggle to decipher exact technical expectations and rubrics.
03. Scalability Bottlenecks
College placement cells cannot provide 24/7 personalized, one-on-one technical coaching to thousands of candidates.
04. Unstructured Data
Colleges rely solely on CGPA, completely ignoring competitive coding ranks, live hackathons, and soft-skill vectors.
