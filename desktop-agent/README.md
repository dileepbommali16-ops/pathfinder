# Pathfinder 2.0 — Local Laptop Desktop Bridge (`desktop-agent`)

Connects your Windows laptop's **real installed Google Chrome** (`google.com`, `mail.google.com`, `web.whatsapp.com`, `programiz.com`) and **VS Code workspace** to Pathfinder 2.0 on Render & Vercel.

## Quick Start (1-Click on Windows)

1. Double-click **`start_bridge.bat`** (or run `..\.venv\Scripts\python.exe desktop-agent\bridge_agent.py --open-chrome`).
2. The first time real Chrome opens, sign in once to **`dileepbommali16@gmail.com`** — your login session is saved permanently in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PathfinderDASChromeProfile`.
3. Look at the Pathfinder HUD bar: **`💻 Bridge Live`** lights up! Now whether you speak from your **Laptop** or your **Phone**, DAS AI directly controls your real Chrome, real Gmail, and coding workspace.
