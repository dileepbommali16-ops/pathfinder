# Pathfinder Platform Overview & Real-World Mission

## What Pathfinder Is
Pathfinder 2.0 is an autonomous AI career intelligence and campus placement co-pilot engineered for undergraduate engineering students.
It transforms ambiguous, stressful campus placement preparation into a transparent, data-calibrated roadmap backed by actual recruitment telemetry.
Instead of relying on generic advice, Pathfinder analyzes each student's specific academic parameters, coding benchmarks, and portfolio projects to predict placement readiness with 88% model accuracy.
The platform combines a calibrated Machine Learning readiness score, an interactive 6-week technical roadmap, 23 production project blueprints, an ATS resume optimizer, and an autonomous 3D voice mentor named DAS.

## The Real-World Problem
Every year, over 1.5 million engineering students across India graduate, yet fewer than 20% are placed in core or product development roles that match their aspirations.
Students struggle with three crippling real-world hurdles:
1. **Blind Readiness**: Students only discover industry CGPA cutoffs, required DSA depth, and skill gaps during final-year placement drives when it is already too late to course-correct.
2. **Generic, One-Size-Fits-All Advice**: Online roadmaps fail to account for a student's specific branch, backlog history, or target corporate tier (Product MNC Tier-1 vs Service Tier-2).
3. **Black-Box ATS & Resume Rejections**: Over 75% of student resumes are filtered out by automated screening systems before a human recruiter ever sees them due to non-quantifiable bullets and missing tech stack keywords.

## Who It Is For
- **Undergraduate Engineering Students (1st to 4th Year)**: To benchmark their skills, eliminate guesswork, build verified projects, and practice mock technical viva rounds.
- **College Placement Cells & Training Officers (TPOs)**: To track cohort analytics, monitor branch-wise placement percentages, and identify at-risk students before campus drives begin.
- **Mentors & Educators**: To assign structured sprint roadmaps and review candidate progress against real recruitment telemetry.

## How It Helps in Real Life
- **Clarity**: You know your exact standing today and what Tier-1 companies (Amazon, Microsoft, Razorpay) expect.

## The Student Journey: Signup to Placement
1. **Intake & Discovery**: Sign up via Lamp Login and complete the 5-step Onboarding Wizard (branch, CGPA, DSA score, target tier).
2. **Diagnostic Audit**: The Readiness Command Center calculates your calibrated score and flags your primary skill gap.
3. **Structured Sprints**: Follow the 6-Week Roadmap, completing curated algorithmic problems and daily milestones.
4. **Portfolio Development**: Choose and build flagship projects using the step-by-step blueprints and Live Skill Verifier.
5. **Resume Optimization**: Scan and rewrite your CV in the ATS Resume Studio to clear recruitment screening filters.
6. **Mock Viva Defense**: Drill high-pressure technical questions with DAS AI until confidence and conceptual depth are locked in.
7. **Placement Success**: Enter campus recruitment drives with proven technical competence and secure Tier-1 offers.
