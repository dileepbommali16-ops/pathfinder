# Pathfinder Engineering & AI Glossary

Clear, two-tier explanations of fundamental computer science, machine learning, and placement terminology for school-level simplicity and college-level precision.

---

### 1. Machine Learning (ML)
- **School-Level Analogy**: Like teaching a child to recognize fruits by showing them 500 pictures of apples and oranges until they can recognize one on their own, instead of writing down hundreds of rules about color and shape.
- **College-Level Definition**: A subfield of Artificial Intelligence where statistical algorithms learn predictive patterns directly from historical sample data without being explicitly programmed with deterministic rules.

### 2. Random Forest
- **School-Level Analogy**: Asking 100 wise village elders to vote on whether tomorrow will rain based on past seasons; the majority vote is almost always more reliable than asking just one person.
- **College-Level Definition**: An ensemble learning method that constructs a large multitude of decorrelated decision trees during training and outputs the mode or mean prediction, preventing overfitting.

### 3. Large Language Model (LLM)
- **School-Level Analogy**: An ultra-intelligent digital librarian who has read every book in the world and can instantly compose essays, explain tricky math concepts, or converse fluently in any language.
- **College-Level Definition**: A deep neural network architecture (typically based on the Transformer) trained on massive multi-billion-word text corpora using self-attention mechanisms to predict and generate human-like natural language tokens.

### 4. Tokens
- **School-Level Analogy**: Like syllables or Lego blocks of words. The word "friendship" might be split into two blocks: "friend" and "ship".
- **College-Level Definition**: The basic atomic units of data processed by LLMs, representing words, sub-words, or character sequences (roughly 1 token ≈ 0.75 words or 4 characters in English).

### 5. Application Programming Interface (API)
- **School-Level Analogy**: Like a restaurant waiter. You (the client) look at the menu, tell the waiter your order, the waiter carries it to the kitchen (server), and brings back your freshly prepared food (response).
- **College-Level Definition**: A formalized set of protocols, endpoints, and specifications that allow two separate software systems to exchange structured data (commonly over HTTP via JSON).

### 6. Dataset
- **School-Level Analogy**: A school register or giant notebook where every student's attendance, test marks, and homework scores are recorded in neat rows and columns.
- **College-Level Definition**: A structured collection of structured or unstructured data points organized in tables, rows, and columns used for statistical modeling, training, or benchmarking.

### 7. Cohort
- **School-Level Analogy**: Your batch or class of students who joined school in the same year and will graduate together.
- **College-Level Definition**: A group of individuals who share a common statistical demographic characteristic or life event during a specific time span (e.g. the 2024 Computer Science graduation batch).

### 8. Applicant Tracking System (ATS)
- **School-Level Analogy**: A strict security guard at an examination hall gate who checks if you have your hall ticket, proper black pen, and ID card before allowing you inside.
- **College-Level Definition**: Automated enterprise recruitment software that parses, indexes, and ranks submitted resumes against target job descriptions using keyword matching and formatting rubrics before human HR review.
