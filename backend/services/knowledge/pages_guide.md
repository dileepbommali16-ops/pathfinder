# Pathfinder Full Pages & Components Guide

## 1. Dashboard (`/dashboard`)
- **Purpose**: Central command center for the student.
- **What the student sees**: Overall placement readiness snapshot, quick stats, active project status, and shortcut cards.
- **How to use**: View your daily score, jump into active modules, or check recommended next actions.

## 2. AI Mock Interview - Live (`/mock-interview`)
- **Purpose**: Real-time voice and video mock interview practice with AI evaluators.
- **What the student sees**: Live video feed, real-time speech transcription, question prompts, and instant behavioral/technical feedback.
- **How to use**: Click start, answer technical/HR questions out loud, and get instant scored analysis on communication, confidence, and correctness.

## 3. Readiness Command - Instant (`/readiness`)
- **Purpose**: Deep ML-powered analysis of student placement probability.
- **What the student sees**: Random Forest prediction percentage, confidence intervals, key strengths, and immediate bottleneck alerts.
- **How to use**: Review input metrics, inspect feature importance charts, and run instant recalculations when skills are updated.

## 4. 6-Week Roadmap - Sprint (`/roadmap`)
- **Purpose**: High-intensity, structured week-by-week learning and execution sprint.
- **What the student sees**: Week 1 to Week 6 milestone targets, daily task checkboxes, resource links, and completion progress bars.
- **How to use**: Follow the sprint tasks week by week, check off completed milestones, and keep your career trajectory on track.

## 5. DAS AI Coach - Voice (`/voice-agent` or Website-wide Floating Orb)
- **Purpose**: The core website-wide voice-first intelligent mentor ("DAS AI").
- **What the student sees**: Floating 3D blue-cyan wireframe head model / audio waveform orb, collapsible chat/voice log panel, and mic/audio controls.
- **How to use**: Click or speak to interact naturally. Ask questions about the platform, request step-by-step project guides (one step at a time), or navigate the site by voice without ever resetting conversation state.

## 6. Project Blueprints (`/projects`)
- **Purpose**: Comprehensive, branch-specific project building guides.
- **What the student sees**: Small, Medium, and Hard project cards across AIML, CSE/IT, ECE/IoT, etc., with step-by-step implementation instructions.
- **How to use**: Select your branch and difficulty, read Step 1, verify completion, and ask DAS AI for Step 2.

## 7. Cohort Analytics (`/cohort-analytics`)
- **Purpose**: Peer comparison and batch-level trend analysis.
- **What the student sees**: Comparative graphs showing batch average readiness, top skill distributions, and hiring trends.
- **How to use**: Benchmark your profile against cohort averages to identify competitive edges.

## 8. ATS Resume Studio (`/resume`)
- **Purpose**: Professional resume parser and ATS optimization suite.
- **What the student sees**: File upload dropzone, ATS score breakdown gauge, keyword gap analysis, and before/after bullet point comparisons.
- **How to use**: Upload PDF/DOCX resume, review extracted skill matches, apply suggested bullet rewrites, and download the optimized version.

## 9. Branches & Courses - Dept (`/branches`)
- **Purpose**: Department-specific curriculum and elective guides (CSE, IT, AIML, CSD, ECE, EEE, Mech, Civil).
- **What the student sees**: Branch overview, core subjects, recommended electives, and career tracks.
- **How to use**: Filter by your department to explore curated academic and career pathways.

## 10. Project Defense (`/project-defense`)
- **Purpose**: Technical viva and cross-questioning simulation for student projects.
- **What the student sees**: Panel simulation interface where AI acts as a senior technical reviewer asking architecture and code defense questions.
- **How to use**: Submit your project repository link or description and defend your design choices against rigorous technical probing.

## 11. Profile & Settings - SSOT (`/profile`)
- **Purpose**: Single Source of Truth (SSOT) for all user data, credentials, and preferences.
- **What the student sees**: Personal details, target roles, academic records, API configurations, and account security controls.
- **How to use**: Update your target career goals, manage your verified badges, and adjust voice/theme preferences.
